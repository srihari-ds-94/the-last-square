Privacy Policy for The Last Square

Effective Date: November 2026

The Last Square ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy outlines our data handling practices.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Last Square is built client-side. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data such as names, email addresses, phone contacts, or physical locations.

2. On-Device Storage

Your game progress, unlocked chapters, stars, and theme preferences are saved locally on your device via standard local storage. This information never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Advertising Frameworks

If you choose to watch rewarded videos for hints, standard anonymized ad providers (e.g., Google AdMob) may serve video clips under their respective terms. No personal identifiers are shared.

4. Contact

For questions or support, contact us at: support@thelastsquare.app
